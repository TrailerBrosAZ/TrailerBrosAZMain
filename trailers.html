RawMaxx UTX

Heavy-duty tandem axleUtility Trailer

Available to rent
1 / 8 photos

From trail weekends to projects around the house, the UTX is a versatile all-around hauler. Its 14-foot × 83-inch deck is a great size for side-by-sides, golf carts, quads, mowers, and everyday cargo. Side rails and a reinforced rear drop gate make loading practical, while the front toolbox keeps the included straps close at hand.

What would you haul?

  • Side-by-sides, golf carts, quads, and other recreational gear
  • Furniture, appliances, and household moves
  • Mowers, landscaping supplies, and yard cleanup loads

Choose the UTX for a trail trip, a golf cart move, or a workday haul that fits its deck and payload. Its manageable size, side rails, and rear gate make it a flexible choice at $60 per day. Check your vehicle’s overall dimensions and loaded weight for the right fit.

RawMaxx SLX

Heavy-duty tandem axleEquipment & Car Hauler

Coming soon
1 / 7 photos

Built around a 20-foot platform, the SLX gives you more room for vehicles and equipment. Drive-over fenders provide a wider loading surface, while pullout ramps and tie-down points throughout the deck support a range of hauling setups. A front-mounted toolbox adds storage, and the rock guard helps shield the load from road debris.

What would you haul?

  • Cars and project vehicles within the trailer’s fit and weight limits
  • Side-by-sides and wider recreational vehicles
  • Compact equipment with a verified loaded weight within capacity

Choose the SLX when your load calls for more deck length, drive-over fenders, or the higher payload capacity. The listed 102-inch width includes the fenders; it is not the clear width between them.
