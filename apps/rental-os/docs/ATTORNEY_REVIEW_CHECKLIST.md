# Arizona attorney review checklist

Use this checklist to document counsel's decisions for a specific agreement version. **This is an issue list, not legal advice or legal approval.** Record unresolved items and required wording changes; do not mark the Rental OS release gate approved until counsel approves the exact final source.

## Agreement and electronic evidence

- [ ] Parties, trailer identity, rental period, charges, deposit, included equipment, and fulfillment are accurately stated.
- [ ] Electronic-record consent, electronic-signature method, attribution, intent to sign, record retention, customer copy, and ability to download/print meet applicable requirements.
- [ ] Printed name, acknowledgment timestamps, signature evidence, source hash, document hash, and audit history are sufficient for the intended evidentiary purpose.
- [ ] Agreement receipt, expiration, re-signing after a material change, and immutable signed-copy rules are adequate.
- [ ] Inspection-choice wording is neutral and informed; affirmative decline is fair and does not imply an overbroad waiver.

## Renter, towing, insurance, and use

- [ ] Minimum age 25 and named-renter-only towing rule are enforceable and clearly disclosed.
- [ ] Tow vehicle, hitch ball, electric brake controller, load, securement, and safe-operation duties are accurate.
- [ ] Insurance acknowledgment states what is required without falsely claiming verification or coverage.
- [ ] Interstate disclosure/owner approval and international prohibition are clear.
- [ ] Prohibited use, payload/GVWR language, plate-verification qualifier, breakdown/accident reporting, and return duties are complete.

## Pricing, payment, cancellation, and collections

- [ ] $60/24 hours, $10 additional hour with $60 partial-day cap, $10/day dolly, included straps, and delivery zones are described without ambiguity.
- [ ] No-separate-tax-line presentation is compliant and not misleading.
- [ ] Full-charge timing, $100 refundable deposit, authorization/collection timing, inspection-based release, and partial damage retention are legally and operationally consistent.
- [ ] 48-hour cancellation, late cancellation, no-show, applicable rental refund, $100 retention, rescheduling, extension, and no early-return proration are approved.
- [ ] Late return, unauthorized extension, failed payment, chargeback, collections, card authorization, and recovery-cost language is approved or removed.
- [ ] Refund timing and payment-provider disclosures are accurate and do not promise outcomes the system cannot prove.

## Condition, damage, delivery, and disputes

- [ ] Pickup inspection offer/decline, return inspection, damage documentation, ordinary wear, valuation, customer notice, and dispute opportunity are fair.
- [ ] Deposit-retain process requires adequate evidence, reason, amount, and customer communication.
- [ ] Delivery approval, distance zones, address handling, failed routing, handoff, and online unavailability beyond 35 miles are sufficiently disclosed.
- [ ] Governing law, Maricopa County venue, dispute process, indemnity, waiver, limitation of liability, repossession, default, attorney fees, and collection remedies are enforceable and appropriately scoped.
- [ ] Any prohibited, unconscionable, misleading, or regulated wording is identified for removal or revision.

## Privacy, records, and consumer disclosures

- [ ] Privacy notice covers customer identity/contact, trip/tow details, agreement/signature evidence, payment identifiers, inspection/damage records, delivery address/distance, and audit logs.
- [ ] Seven-year agreement/financial/inspection retention and 90-day raw driver-license-photo deletion policy are approved and operationally supportable.
- [ ] Customer access/correction/deletion rights, legal holds, breach response, processor disclosures, and cross-service transfers are addressed.
- [ ] Email/secure-link consent, delivery evidence, accessibility, language, consumer notices, and record-copy requirements are addressed.
